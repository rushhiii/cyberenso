# The ensō and the design

## A circle made in one breath

An ensō is traditionally painted with one continuous brushstroke. The stroke
is allowed to show pressure, speed, texture, and an intentional opening.
Those qualities make it a record of a moment rather than a polished
geometric symbol.

CyberEnsō preserves those ideas in a digital medium:

- **Open arcs** keep the mark from becoming a mathematically perfect ring.
- **Layered paths** suggest the changing pressure of a brush.
- **Hash-derived variation** makes each text input produce its own gesture.
- **A stable seed** means the gesture can be revisited rather than lost.
- **SVG output** keeps the mark expressive and sharp at every scale.

The interface uses a quiet paper palette, restrained borders, and generous
space around the mark. The UI should feel like a frame for the gesture, not
another focal point.
