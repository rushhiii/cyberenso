CyberEnsō is a contemporary digital interpretation of the Zen ensō: a circle painted in one spontaneous brushstroke.

What is an ensō?

An ensō (円相, “circle form”) is a Zen ink painting. Its open shape can hold ideas of emptiness, fullness, imperfection, movement, and the state of the person who made it. The gesture is more important than geometric perfection.

How the hashing works

The editor runs locally. Each character in your text is folded into a 32-bit FNV-1a-style hash. That number becomes the seed for a small pseudo-random generator. The generator chooses the radius, start point, and sweep of each layered arc.

  • Same input → same seed → same Ensō.
  • A changed word → a changed seed → a new gesture.
  • Thirty-four open SVG paths create the brush-like weight.
  • The SVG stays sharp at any size and can be downloaded directly.

Privacy by design

There is no backend. Text is never sent to an API. Saved history uses only this browser's localStorage, and you can clear it through your browser settings at any time.
